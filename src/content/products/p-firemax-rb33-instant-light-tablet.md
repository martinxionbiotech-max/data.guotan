## Form & Use

Instant-light round tablet — the fast-lighting end of the quick-light
class: a single flame brings it to burn in 15–50 seconds. Typical for
hookah lounges that need speed and for incense use.

Note: the datasheet prints ash content as `0.045`. That figure is almost
certainly a formatting error for 4.5%; it is recorded here verbatim and
flagged rather than silently corrected.
